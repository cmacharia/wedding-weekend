Film · Opening Night

Cinema Columbus Film Festival

Opening night kicks off with Origins: Shorts of Ohio, a block of four short films by Ohio filmmakers, followed by a Q&A. The festival runs through Sunday across venues citywide.

On view 7:00 PM Gateway Film Center
TH·02
Live Music

Summer Music Series at Stories on High

The season's closing set of the live series, moving from classic jazz to rock and roll. Tonight's bill: the George Barrie Band.

On view 8:00 – 11:00 PM Stories on High
TH·03
Music · Free Admission

BAM Thursdays

Bar, Art, and Music in the Sculpture Garden — a featured local artist from 6–8 PM, DJs from 5–10 PM, and free general admission to the galleries all evening.

On view 5:00 – 10:00 PM Columbus Museum of Art
TH·04
Live Jazz

Off the Record: Jazz Night at the Quarry

A weekly Thursday tradition — intimate live jazz, craft cocktails, and lake views at Bada Bean Bada Booze.

On view 7:00 – 9:00 PM Quarry Trails
TH·05
Outdoor Movie

NightLight Outdoor Movie Series — La La Land

A screening under the open sky, part of the traveling NightLight outdoor series.

On view Evening, at dusk Columbus
TH·06
Speakeasy · Bar

Bar Room at the Pizzuti

A third-floor speakeasy inspired by the rum bars of the Bahamas, linking Columbus to the Caribbean through Afro-Caribbean music and the living legacies of the African diaspora in Central Ohio.

On view 2:00 – 8:00 PM The Pizzuti
